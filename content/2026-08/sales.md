### The unassigned gap

{{ m("sales.unassigned_note") }}

Records that reach no rep arrive email-only, almost never carry a phone
number, and convert at about {{ m("sales.unassigned_rate_14mo") }} over the
last fourteen months against forty to seventy percent for assigned leads. This
is a NetSuite routing-automation gap, not a rep issue, and it is the single
largest lever on the pipeline that costs no media money. Reconciling it is
manual work on the marketing side; it is in progress.

## Lead quality

{{ m("sales.quality_note") }}

Two phone-capture figures are shown, and they are different things. The
**blended** figure counts every record created. The **received-by-rep** figure
counts only records routed to one of you — and that is much higher, because
the unassigned records are the ones without phone numbers. The blended number
understates what you actually get.
