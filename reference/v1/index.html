About this hub

All three dashboards pull from the same underlying NetSuite + marketing data sources. They reflect the same rolling 12-month window (June 2025 – May 2026) but are tailored to different audiences. Methodology and data lineage are documented in the marketing analysis workbook.

Data sources & filters applied

  • NetSuite — customers, revenue, lead quality, ship-to states
  • Marketing Spend — NetSuite income statement (GL 66212)
  • Google Search Console · Google Ads · GA4 · Google My Business · Hotjar
  • Subsidiary = VHPC LLC. Customer Category excludes GarageExperts and Vendor.
  • Customer = stage starts with "Customer-" AND has a first sale date populated.
  • All revenue figures are NET — product only, excluding shipping and tax, with returns and credit memos deducted.

Refresh cadence

Dashboards are refreshed monthly. URLs stay the same on each refresh — bookmark them once and the latest data will load each time.
