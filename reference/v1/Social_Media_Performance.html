Watch items + recommendations

What to act on this month

A short list of things the data surfaces. Color coded by urgency.

🟡
LinkedIn reach is down three months running — May was the peakImpressions: 2,606 (May) → 2,113 (Jun) → 1,097 (Jul) → 370 (Aug through the 18th). That's 58% off the peak. Engagement rate is actually at a record 21.1% in August, so the audience that still sees the content is more engaged than ever — the problem is reach and cadence, not content quality. Follower growth has stalled too: 189 in June, 13 in July, 0 so far in August. Recommend returning to the 5–7 post/month cadence that produced the May peak.
🔵
Pickleball post template was the breakout — replicate itThe same pickleball content performed best on both Instagram (542 views, highest of the month) and LinkedIn (43% engagement rate). The pattern: real customer install + specific product spec + clean project photos. Aim for 2-3 of these case-study posts per month across both platforms.
🟡
Instagram reach dropped 79% in April — investigateReach fell from 20,220 in March to 4,180 in April. Profile views also down 36%. Check: did posting cadence drop? Did a specific viral post in March account for most of that 20K, and April is just regression to mean? Worth pulling daily IG data for both months to see if it's content-frequency related.
🟡
Two unexplained follower spikes with no matching posts — March (74) and June (189)February: 6 new followers. March: 74 despite zero posts. June: 189 on 5 posts, then July back to 13 and August to 0. Both spikes are far outside the normal 6–29 range. Possible causes: a rep linking the page from their profile, an external tag, or a trade-show/partner referral. Worth 15 minutes in LinkedIn's follower-source report — if either spike has a repeatable cause, it's the cheapest growth lever on the page.
🟡
Instagram is giving back the giveaway followersFollowers peaked at 7,015 at the end of July after the giveaway added 155. As of August 18 the count is 6,903 — a net loss of 112, which is more than the giveaway gained. Giveaway follower churn is normal, but it means the +155 headline should not be treated as durable audience growth. Profile views are holding up better (11,929 in the first 18 days, tracking near July's 29,755 pace).
🔵
Test Instagram format diversityAll 4 top April posts were carousels. Reels reach tends to be 5-10x higher than feed posts on Instagram in 2026. Worth running a 30-day test: 50% reels, 50% carousels. Track which format earns higher reach per post.
🔵
Meta Ads budget shifted down 28% in April — was that intentional?April spend was $2,514 vs $3,500/mo earlier. CPM rose to $12.30 (vs $7.53-10.78 prior months) suggesting less efficient auctions at lower budget. If the budget cut was intentional, fine. If accidental (campaign ran out, cap hit), worth refilling.
